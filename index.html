FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What plumbing problems does Tubero Experts handle?

Tubero Experts handles three kinds of work: general plumbing (repairs and pipe installation), declogging of drains and lines, and leak repair.

How do I book a plumber?

Call or text 0999-117-4009, or send the quote form on this page with your name, number and a short description of the problem.

What should I do while waiting for the plumber to fix a leak?

Close the shut-off valve nearest the leak, or the main water valve if you can’t find one. Switch off electricity to any area where water is reaching outlets or appliances, and catch dripping water with a pail.

Why does my drain keep clogging?

Repeat clogs usually come from grease, food scraps, hair or soap build-up deeper in the line, not just at the drain opening. Clearing only the top can bring the clog back, so the whole line may need to be cleared.

Should I use chemical drain cleaners first?

Strong chemical cleaners can damage older pipes and are dangerous to handle, and they often fail on grease or solid blockages. If a plunger doesn’t work, it’s safer to call a plumber.

What information should I have ready when I call?

Your location, which fixture or area has the problem (sink, toilet, floor drain, pipe), when it started, and photos if you can take them. This helps the team come prepared.
